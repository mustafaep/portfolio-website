## A heading that implies a question

Open each section with the answer. The first sentence should still make sense
when it is lifted out of the page and quoted on its own, because that is
usually all a search engine or a language model will take.

Then expand: the context, the constraint, what you tried, what you chose.

## Adding a new post

Create `content/blog/en/<slug>.mdx` and `content/blog/tr/<slug>.mdx`, fill in
the frontmatter above, and drop the `draft` line. The route, sitemap entry,
RSS item and JSON-LD are generated from the file — nothing else needs editing.
